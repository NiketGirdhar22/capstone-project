## C. Thematic Literature Synthesis

### C.1 Explainability in Predictive Maintenance
Recent works confirm a clear shift from pure accuracy toward interpretable maintenance decisions. Paper [1] operationalizes LIME/SHAP/PDP/ICE together and demonstrates practical feature-attribution usage for rotating systems. Paper [16] extends this line by linking interpretable RUL outputs to maintenance scheduling decisions.

### C.2 Telemetry-Based RUL Modeling
Across [4], [10], [12], [13], [17], and [25], the dominant pattern is hybrid temporal modeling:
- LSTM/attention remains strong for C-MAPSS-like telemetry.
- Transformer and TCN variants improve long-horizon pattern extraction.
- CNN front-ends improve local feature extraction before temporal modules.
These findings align well with your telemetry subsystem design (global models + specialist routing).

### C.3 Operating-Condition Shift and Domain Adaptation
Papers [7], [14], and [15] emphasize the same practical bottleneck observed in your project: regime variability distorts degradation signals. Change-point aware modeling and domain adaptation are consistently used to stabilize generalization under changing conditions.

### C.4 Acoustic Anomaly and Motor Health Diagnostics
Papers [2] and [20] support your acoustic path directly:
- High-frequency and time-frequency representations are highly informative.
- Temporal sequence models (GRU/CNN hybrids) capture non-stationary fault cues.
Paper [8] further indicates that semi-supervised setups are useful when abnormal labels are scarce.

### C.5 Multimodal Fusion for Fault Diagnosis and PHM
Papers [5], [6], [9], [18], [19], [21], and [22] show clear gains from multimodal fusion versus single-sensor baselines. Recent progress also moves toward trustworthy fusion and adaptive MoE frameworks, matching your capstone's fusion-layer motivation.

### C.6 Reviews and Strategic Positioning
Survey/review papers [11], [23], and [24] collectively reinforce three trends:
- Deep models are now baseline for PHM.
- Transfer/domain adaptation is critical for deployment.
- Explainability and reliability constraints are becoming first-class requirements.

## D. Research Gaps (for your Chapter 2.6)

1. Most RUL papers still evaluate in single-domain settings; cross-condition robustness remains limited.
2. Acoustic and telemetry streams are often studied separately; integrated fusion benchmarks are still sparse.
3. Explainability is frequently post-hoc but rarely tied to maintenance policy optimization.
4. Many multimodal fault studies focus on classification; fewer jointly model diagnosis plus RUL regression.
5. Public benchmarks for true multimodal predictive maintenance (telemetry + acoustics + operational metadata) remain underdeveloped.
